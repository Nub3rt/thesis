\chapter{Introduction}
\label{intro}

\href{https://fryxgames.se/product/terraforming-mars/}{\textit{Terraforming Mars}} is a board game for 1 to 5 players. In it, the players control corporations which race to terraform the planet Mars. To achieve this, they must raise the temperature, form oceans and add oxygen to the atmosphere, and alongside these they create settlements, plant forest and much more.

The gameplay consist of generations, and each generation has three phases: research phase, action phase and production phase. In the research phase, players can choose from a few cards that represent projects contributing to the taming of Mars. In the action phase, players can use these cards, use actions on specific played cards, or perform standard projects. In the production phase, players get to replenish their resources based on their production values. Mars is represented by a hexagonal grid, on which players can place oceans, greeneries and cities.

The goal of this thesis was to implement this game with the ability to easily expand it: there are a variety of board variants, expansions, decks which the players can choose from. The other goal was to develop the application in a high performance, highly customizable environment supporting 3D rendering and models. Therefore, C++ and OpenGL were chosen for this project.
